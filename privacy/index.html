Privacy

What you draw and your house stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

A challenge link carries your character after the #. That part of a link never reaches our server; whoever you send it to can see the character.

We count anonymous page events (for example "a duel started") with a random number made fresh for each visit. No cookies, no accounts, no IP addresses kept.

The Spriteer app has its own policy in the App Store listing. Questions: hello@swiftian.com.
